# Delete Account

Account deletion is permanent.

## Before Deleting

Download any records you may need before deleting the account. BarnBuddy's PDF exports cover selected farm and animal records; they are not a complete account-data export, and some exports require Premium.

If you have a paid Premium subscription, open **Account Settings → Subscription → Manage billing** and confirm cancellation with the billing provider first. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions.

## Delete Your Account

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Open **Account settings**.
3. Find the delete account action.
4. Read the confirmation, including the separate subscription reminder, and confirm the deletion.

BarnBuddy asks Clerk to remove the sign-in and deletes linked BarnBuddy database records. Cleanup of uploaded files is a separate provider request and can fail; some chapter advisor contact details and admin activity history are stored independently and may remain. If deletion appears incomplete, contact [BarnBuddy support](https://barnbuddy.pro/contact).

BarnBuddy shows a confirmation when the deletion request completes. Do not assume third-party records or backups are immediately erased.
