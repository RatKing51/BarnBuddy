# BarnBuddy design guide

## Visual direction

Use the current BarnBuddy website as the starting point. Someone returning to check a herd or compare plans should recognize the site and know where to go. Keep the wordmark, familiar colors, page structure, and existing wording. Give the site a more individual look through typography, spacing, alignment, and the way it presents its own photos and screenshots.

## Design around the content

- On the homepage, give the existing product screenshots enough room for someone to read the interface. Align each screenshot with the text that explains it.
- In the founder section, let the photograph and Gage's own words carry the story. A readable text column and a simple caption are enough.
- In feature lists, try aligned headings, open space, and a divider before putting every item in a rounded card. Keep a visible boundary where it helps someone recognize a clickable item or follow a record.
- Use the existing accent color to draw attention to the main action. Decorative gradients, badges, glows, and entrance effects can be removed where they compete with the content.
- Keep heading sizes and spacing consistent across pages. On phones, keep related text, images, and controls together in reading order.

## Review the refresh

Compare the updated page with the current version on desktop and mobile. Check that the same information and actions are easy to find, product images are readable, and links and forms work with a keyboard. Respect existing theme and reduced-motion preferences.
