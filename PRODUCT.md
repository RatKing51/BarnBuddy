# BarnBuddy product brief
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web.

## Product Purpose

BarnBuddy helps livestock owners keep track of their animals, herds, health records, and projects.

## Users

Small livestock operations, FFA and 4-H project owners, and chapter advisors use BarnBuddy. Keep the current balance between individual livestock records and chapter features.

## Scope of the refresh

Keep the existing pages, section order, copy, navigation, and features. The goal is a familiar BarnBuddy site with a more considered visual presentation. DESIGN.md describes how to apply that direction.

## Content and behavior

Use the existing product screenshots, founder photograph, founder story, and published news and reviews. Preserve the public content feed and its bundled fallbacks so updates continue to appear. Keep pricing, sign-in, account actions, chapter membership, and member-controlled project sharing working as they do today.
